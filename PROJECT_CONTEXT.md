# 1. Project Overview

Beyond the Space Perfume is a Dubai-based perfume business requiring a premium, modern and fully functional website.

The website must present a large inventory of approximately 1,000 perfumes, support both English and Arabic, and provide an elegant browsing experience for customers worldwide.

The design should communicate luxury, professionalism and exclusivity while remaining approachable and easy to navigate.

The objective is to develop the complete website and transfer ownership to the client after delivery.

**This is a one-time website development and sale project, not a recurring website maintenance service.**

The immediate priority is producing a convincing, interactive website demonstration within three days.

The demo should demonstrate the proposed design, functionality and customer experience without requiring the entire production website to be completed.

# 4. Inventory and Product Data Strategy

## 4.1 Final Inventory

The client is expected to provide the actual product inventory for Beyond the Space Perfume.

We have not received this inventory yet.

This is not a blocker for the demo.

The final website must be capable of supporting approximately 1,000 perfume products.

## 4.2 Temporary Demo Inventory

An existing De Fragnancia inventory list contains approximately 600 perfume entries.

This list will act as the temporary data source for development and demonstration, subject to permission to reuse the data and product images.

Potential fields include:
- Brand name
- Perfume name
- Product variant
- Bottle size
- Perfume concentration
- Gender classification
- Product price
- Product image
- Product description
- Fragrance notes
- Product category

These fields are proposed data-model fields, not a claim that every column is already available in the existing spreadsheet.

We will inspect and normalize the original list before importing it.

## 4.3 Data Preparation Workflow

1. Inspect the available spreadsheet.
2. Identify existing product columns.
3. Clean inconsistent names and formats.
4. Remove genuine duplicate records without merging distinct sizes or variants.
5. Standardize brand, size and concentration values.
6. Identify missing images, descriptions and prices.
7. Produce a consistent product-data structure.
8. Import representative products into the demo.
9. Test search, filters and product-detail views.
10. Validate that the eventual bulk import can support all 1,000 products.

The demo does not require entering all 600 products manually.

We can use 20–30 representative perfumes for visual development and import additional clean records if time permits.

## 4.4 Product Data Replacement

When Beyond the Space Perfume provides its actual inventory:

- Replace temporary product records.
- Import the client's genuine product descriptions, prices and photos.
- Verify product categories and variations.
- Update language-specific product information.
- Remove temporary demonstration material.
- Validate product counts, search results and filters.

Temporary data must never accidentally be represented as confirmed Beyond the Space Perfume inventory.

# 5. Website Design Direction

## 5.1 Overall Style

The design should follow a light luxury aesthetic.

Recommended characteristics:
- Ivory, off-white and soft cream backgrounds.
- Warm beige and restrained gold accents.
- Deep charcoal text.
- Elegant typography.
- Spacious layouts.
- Large perfume photography.
- Subtle scrolling transitions.
- Clean product cards.
- Minimal visual clutter.
- Smooth interactions.

The aesthetic should resemble a contemporary luxury fragrance boutique rather than a crowded discount marketplace.

## 5.2 Design Principles

**Luxury through simplicity:** Large photography, typography and negative space should create the premium feel.

**Product-first:** Perfume images, brands, prices and key characteristics must be easy to identify.

**Consistency:** The homepage, catalogue, product pages and Arabic version should share the same visual language.

**Mobile-first usability:** The website should feel polished on both smartphones and desktop browsers.

**Functional animations:** Animations should enhance the experience without slowing navigation.

## 5.3 Branding Inputs

The final brand design should incorporate:
- Beyond the Space Perfume's approved logo.
- Its official brand colours, if provided.
- Approved product images.
- Brand descriptions and promotional content.
- Official social-media and contact information.

Where these assets are unavailable, temporary clearly marked placeholders may be used for the demo.

# 7. English and Arabic Requirements

The website must support both English and Arabic.

Language support involves more than translating navigation buttons.

## English

The English version uses a conventional left-to-right layout.

## Arabic

The Arabic version must include:
- Right-to-left layout.
- Correct text alignment.
- Appropriate Arabic typography.
- Translated navigation.
- Translated interface labels.
- Correct dropdown and menu behavior.
- Arabic-friendly form controls.
- Proper mobile navigation.
- Language-specific product content where supplied or approved.

## Language Switching

A visible language selector should allow visitors to switch between English and Arabic.

Ideally, visitors should remain on the equivalent page when changing languages.

For example, changing languages while viewing a perfume should not unexpectedly send the visitor to the homepage.

Automated translations can accelerate preparation, but important customer-facing Arabic text should be reviewed before final delivery.

# 8. WhatsApp Enquiry System

The existing base-plan direction uses WhatsApp enquiries.

Instead of completing online payment, a customer discovers a perfume and contacts the business.

Example journey:

1. Customer opens the website.
2. Customer searches for a perfume.
3. Customer selects a product.
4. Customer checks the description, size and price.
5. Customer clicks the WhatsApp enquiry button.
6. WhatsApp opens with a prepared message mentioning the product.

Example message:

"Hello, I'm interested in [Perfume Name], [Size]. Could you please confirm availability and ordering details?"

The message can be localized for Arabic visitors.

The client will eventually provide the official WhatsApp number.

No payment gateway is required for this confirmed catalogue enquiry flow.

# 9. Proposed Technical Architecture

This is a recommendation, not a finalized technical decision.

For the three-day demo, the proposed development stack is:

| Layer | Proposed Technology |
|---|---|
| Frontend | Next.js |
| Language | TypeScript |
| Styling | Tailwind CSS |
| UI components | shadcn/ui |
| Animations | Motion |
| Localization | next-intl |
| Demo product data | JSON derived from inventory |
| Demo deployment | Vercel |

Reasons for this approach:
- Fast custom interface development.
- Reusable components.
- Strong responsive layouts.
- Support for English and Arabic.
- Flexible product-data integration.
- Straightforward preview deployments.
- Existing developer familiarity with Next.js.

## Final Production Architecture

The production stack has not been chosen yet.

A custom Next.js website could use a headless CMS and managed database.

Alternatively, WordPress and WooCommerce catalogue mode could provide established inventory-management tools with a less custom administrative implementation.

The final decision should account for:
- Long-term independence of the owner.
- Ease of managing 1,000 perfumes.
- Bulk product imports.
- Arabic support.
- Client hosting preferences.
- Security updates.
- Future extension requirements.
- Cost of third-party infrastructure.
- Ease of complete handover.

**Architecture principle:** Do not make the client's business dependent on the original developer for ordinary operations.

# 15. Project Risks and Preventive Measures

### Risk: Demo scope becomes too large

Mitigation: Prioritize complete customer journeys over building every production feature.

### Risk: Temporary inventory is incomplete

Mitigation: Use a clean representative dataset with safe placeholders and build the importer separately.

### Risk: Arabic is visually broken

Mitigation: Test RTL layout from the beginning rather than adding it at the end.

### Risk: Demo looks polished but does not work

Mitigation: Ensure essential buttons, links, routes and filters operate correctly.

### Risk: Final website cannot be managed independently

Mitigation: Choose an owner-friendly CMS or admin system before final production architecture is committed.

### Risk: Third-party assets or client data cannot legally be reused

Mitigation: Confirm permission for temporary inventory and images; use licensed placeholders if necessary.

### Risk: Client assumes additional features are included

Mitigation: Clearly distinguish agreed functionality, demonstrated functionality and optional future features.

# 16. Development Principles

1. Build a visually convincing but functional demo first.
2. Do not attempt to complete all 1,000 production product records during the demo phase.
3. Keep temporary inventory independent from final client inventory.
4. Support English and Arabic architecturally from the beginning.
5. Treat mobile as a first-class experience.
6. Preserve a clean and scalable product schema.
7. Build reusable components.
8. Avoid unnecessary integrations during the three-day deadline.
9. Avoid permanent dependencies on developer-owned accounts.
10. Ensure the eventual website can be operated by Beyond the Space Perfume without ongoing developer involvement.
11. Do not confuse Beyond the Space Perfume with De Fragnancia.
12. Do not assume the website price is fixed.
13. Distinguish production-ready functionality from demonstration-only functionality.
