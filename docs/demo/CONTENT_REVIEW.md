# Demo Content Review

Reviewed: 2026-10-26

## Final selection

100 unique fragrance records across 40 houses: 38 Men, 10 Women, 52 Unisex. The early gender mix targets were not allowed to override source reliability; the strict no-warning pool contained only 10 distinct women's records. No unknown gender was relabelled. Exactly eight records are featured.

Selection is recorded, in order, in `content/curation.json`; per-ID source photo/condition/representation is in `content/provenance.json`. Every selected record is a single-bottle perfume, exact source match, positive size, known gender, with no source warning. Duplicate fragrance/concentration identities use one size. Testers may supply reference fragrance data, but no tester/retail packaging claim is made in this demo. This is not a confirmed Beyond the Space stock list.

## Image inspection

Opened and directly inspected all five original contact sheets covering the initial 100 selected photographs. Checked bottle/label identity, legibility, cropping, obvious logo-only or wrong-format images, and layout consistency. Rejected campaign-only images P0064, P0065, P0066, P0068, P0069 and P0072: bottles were too small on red backgrounds for the intended browsing presentation. Replaced them with the remaining eligible records, retaining exactly 100.

Reviewed the updated final sheet 5, including the six newly selected records: P0443, P0466, P0468, P0470, P0521 and P0533. The previously viewed photos in the earlier sheets were retained, just reordered after the exclusions. The generated files are `artifacts/content/sheet-1.jpg` through `sheet-5.jpg`.

Some selected reference assets retain lifestyle compositions (notably Lesquendieu and regional discovery imagery). These are reference photographs, not claims of supplied packaging. Low-resolution source imagery is not upscaled into invented detail. Source photo labels/captions remain truthful; all 100 prepared files were decoded/processed by Pillow and passed the local-file audit.

Near-white connected backdrops were made transparent for more consistent product stages; original source files were untouched. Enclosed bottle/label whites are preserved. Reviewed rendered hero, featured cards, catalogue and detail screenshots after processing. Product image failure has a usable text fallback.

## Copy

No commercial source prices, comparison-store links, supplier costs, business claims, stock statuses or invented note pyramids were imported. Descriptions are short editorial introductions based on retained family tags, not independent fragrance verification. Notes without a corresponding draft Arabic translation are omitted from both languages; up to six retained notes are shown.

Official names remain Latin. Arabic UI, descriptions, families, concentrations, notes, disclosures and WhatsApp messages are drafted and rendered in RTL. Client language review is still required before production.

User-confirmed photo-publication permission is recorded in provenance; no independent license or exact-package verification is claimed.
